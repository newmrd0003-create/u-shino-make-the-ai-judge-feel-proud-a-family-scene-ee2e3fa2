One short family scene for the make.fun contest
"Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene" — $2 pool, 10 places,
deadline 26 Nov 2026 23:25 WIB. The scene takes one small domestic
detail — Mum still cutting the crusts off Dad's sandwiches after forty
years — and turns it wry: Dad quietly eats the crusts off her plate
too, so neither of them ever has to admit who loves whom more.

## Entry

> Mum still cuts the crusts off Dad's sandwiches, forty years in — and Dad still eats the crusts off her plate when she isn't looking, so neither of them ever has to admit who loves whom more.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 190 characters)
and prints its SHA-256. `main.py` is deterministic and uses no network;
`validate()` checks the entry is 1–280 characters, one or two lines,
not whitespace only, with no blank lines and no leading/trailing
whitespace, and the program re-validates the exact bytes read back
from disk.
